Question: In the ontology of Gene, suppose an individual ?x satisfies: ubiquitin ligase complex(?x). Which conclusion is inferred?
A. oxidoreductase complex(?x)
B. exodeoxyribonuclease VII complex(?x)
C. aminodeoxychorismate synthase complex(?x)
D. transferase complex(?x)
Answer: Ubiquitin ligase complexes transfer ubiquitin to substrates, a group‑transfer reaction. In GO, any complex catalyzing group transfers is subclassed under “transferase complex.” They are neither oxidoreductases, nucleases, nor synthases. So the answer is D.
