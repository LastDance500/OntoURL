Question: In the ontology of Financial Industry Business, which of the following is a valid domain for the data property 'has extendable maturity date'?
A. common share
B. preferred share
C. dividend
D. preferred dividend
Answer: The data property ‘has extendable maturity date’ applies only to financial instruments capable of deferrable redemption schedules. Common shares have no maturity, and dividends are payment events, not instruments. Preferred shares often include extendable maturity features. So the answer is B.
